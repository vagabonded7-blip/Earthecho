# Earth Echo — NASA Data Source Provenance & Scientific Catalogue

## 1. Schema Version 1.1 Architectural Compliance

In conformance with **Schema v1.1**, all dataset records generated by this pipeline adhere to:
- Multi-dataset manifest registry (`public/data/manifest.json`).
- Frame and channel addressing: `dataset.frames[frameIndex].values[channelId][y][x]`.
- Dataset-specific sonification profiles with explicit audio legend definitions.
- Strict distinction between **image-derived indices** (dimensionless relative intensity $[0, 1]$) and **physical measurements** (calibrated quantities with scientific SI units).
- Clear declaration of coordinate space: image-grid indices ($x \in [0, 63]$, $y \in [0, 31]$), not unverified geographic coordinates.

## 3. Detailed Scientific Provenance: Primary Target (SVS #5313)

### Publication & Metadata
- **Identifier:** NASA SVS #5313 (EIC Version) / SVS #5276 (Global Standard)
- **Title:** Change in Night Lights between 2012 and 2023 - Earth Information Center Version
- **Attribution:** Visualization by Kel Elkins (USRA / NASA SVS), using NASA/NOAA Suomi NPP VIIRS Black Marble data courtesy of Dr. Miguel Román (Leidos) and the NASA Black Marble Science Team.
- **Organization:** NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Earth Information Center
- **Access URL:** https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5313/

### Sensor & Product
- **Satellite:** Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership (Suomi NPP)
- **Instrument:** Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite (VIIRS) Day/Night Band (DNB)
- **Scientific Product:** Black Marble Annual Composite (VNP46 suite)

### Data Semantics & Limitations
- **Extracted Channel:** `brightness`
- **Channel Type:** `image_derived_index`
- **Normalization:** Shared linear scaling $[0, 255] \rightarrow [0.0, 1.0]$.
- **Physical Caveat:** The extracted values represent visual luminance derived from the public NASA animation, incorporating visualization contrast curves. They do not represent raw uncalibrated sensor voltage or unadjusted physical radiance.
- **Polar Mask:** Latitudes beyond approximately $70^\circ \text{N}$ and $60^\circ \text{S}$ (rows $y \le 1$ and $y \ge 29$) are represented as `null` due to solar zenith angle limitations, auroral interference, and permanent ice cover.
